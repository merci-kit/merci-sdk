Lesson 33: The knowledge graph: a contract that can say no

the typed graph: mechanical pass, model enrichment, negative/conditional/evidenced edges, the decision gate, a budgeted answer

1. Why This Feature Exists

A vendor contract has to be read, not just indexed. The mechanical pass needs no model: sections, definitions, obligations, a prohibition and the condition behind the suspension right all land as typed nodes with source pointers. The model adds only the *issue* the reader must answer and the edges that answer it, through knowledge_patch, which is validated before it can touch the graph. The graph then records a rejected reading and a conditional right, and one asserted edge must survive a decision gate before it is admitted.

buildGraph() on a supply agreement; validateGraph(); saveGraph().
B — semantic
the model adds an issue and the depends_on edges that answer it.
C — shapes
a negative edge (the rejected reading), a conditional edge (only under the payment clause), an evidenced edge (one edge, two sources).
D — gate
createDecisionGate() admits or rejects one asserted edge.
E — closed
gatePolicy: 'closed' refuses to read an uncalibrated null verdict as approval.
F — read
a fresh session answers from the budgeted block alone.
